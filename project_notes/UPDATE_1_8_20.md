# DogDex 1.8.20 — EN/DE cameo coverage

Perrin / Gemma TWM 220 is now collectible in English and German, with Hisuian
Growlithe reflected in the camera lens. Both localized cards are Special
Illustration Rare, Full Art, with one explicit holo printing each.

The wider review adds 12 physical targets across seven new card entries and
four existing research entries. Every previous card and printing ID survives.

Existing EN MEP 108, 110 and 92 and DE SM231 research identities are promoted
in place. Their original IDs and old assessment evidence remain available.
Products describe distribution under Products; they create no second collectible.

Eight species links are repaired without adding printings: Pikachu ex 30th 150
gains Zorua, Zoroark and Lycanroc in both ledgers; German Fennekin MEP059 gains
Galarian Zigzagoon; German Zorua SM83 gains Zoroark. Lucario remains on Pikachu.

Paradise Resort MEP092 is non-holo. The provider holo label conflicts with the
exact normal-finish inventory and German physical listing; the rejected claim
is retained in the evidence rather than becoming an extra printing.

A frozen reference registry checks 153 accepted EN/DE appearances and the 12
reviewed physical identities. The import pipeline, patch, read-only project check
and updater validate it. Dropped links, hosts, stamps and duplicate canonical
hosts are rejected. This is positive coverage, not a claim that every artwork or
every historical product variant has been exhaustively verified.

Announced MEP120, German STAFF/rank promos, DLR095 reverse and weak older regional
claims remain unresolved. A German translation or digital face alone does not
confirm a German physical release. JA/KO data and the 41-species roster are unchanged.
All added marketplace IDs remain unconfirmed suggestions; saved matches stay intact.

Generic accepted targets: 4,605 → 4,617. Raw printing records: 4,615 → 4,627.
Entries: 3,832 → 3,839. A personal manual printing can increase the local denominator.

Install on application 1.8.19: close DogDex, extract the update ZIP directly into
the existing DogDex folder, and run UPDATE_DOGDEX.bat. Run CHECK_DOGDEX.bat after
installation. The full Windows ZIP is an alternative fresh-install package, not
another required update. Personal files, runtime, Git and existing viewer are
preserved. Re-export the mobile viewer when ready; nothing is published here.

Facts, source receipts, reference comparison, original hashes and coverage
requirements are in data/research/cameo_1820/. See DATA_SOURCES_1_8_20.md and
VALIDATION_1_8_20.md for the scope and completed checks.
