# Cameo source review — 13 November 2026

This EN/DE review compared 253 appearance claims from three discovery sources,
then assessed English and German availability separately (506 regional cases).
It does not rerun the individual-card printing audit or change Asian ledgers.

| Discovery source | Use | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CardCameos](https://cardcameos.com/) | Species/form artwork leads, including reflections and newer promo art | Deliberately omits many reprints; foreign art and object attributions require review |
| [Pokecameos](https://pokecameos.com/) | A second artwork index and reprint leads | Regional pages/translated names are not physical availability proof |
| [Community master](https://www.elitefourum.com/t/list-of-all-pokemon-tcg-cameos-english/31105) | 41 roster-specific leads extracted from its older set checklist | Published master stops at Crown Zenith; includes a Cynthia SV82 set-heading error |

The two artwork indexes may share upstream data. Agreement between them is not
treated as two independent proofs of a physical regional printing. Localized
card faces, publisher release/distribution information and exact physical tuples
are recorded separately. The master-list SV82 attribution is resolved to Hidden
Fates Shiny Vault, not duplicated into Ultra Prism. Out-of-scope Zygarde forms
are held apart from the selected 10% form.

- [Publisher's German Twilight Masquerade art feature](https://www.pokemon.com/de/pokemon-news/illustrationen-der-pokemon-sammelkartenspiel-erweiterung-karmesin-purpur-maskerade-im-zwielicht): Growlithe reflection on Gemma 220. The direct preparation fetch hit an access challenge; the indexed publisher text and separately acquired localized card faces are distinguished in the receipts.
- [Publisher's Delta Reign card 095](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/pokemon-cards/series/me06/95/) and [German art feature](https://www.pokemon.com/de/features/wirf-einen-blick-auf-karten-der-pokemon-sammelkartenspiel-erweiterung-mega-entwicklung-delta-herrschaft): right-hand stadium panorama, Growlithe. Regional normal-card inventory supports one non-holo; reverse remains unresolved.
- [English November products](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/check-out-every-pokemon-tcg-product-release-in-november-2026) and [German November products](https://www.pokemon.com/de/news/wirf-einen-blick-auf-alle-pokemon-sammelkartenspiel-produkte-die-im-november-2026-veroeffentlicht-werden): released Day/Night Ultra-Premium Collections. Separate EN/DE MEP108 and MEP110 faces identify background Pokémon and the anniversary logo.
- [Publisher's 2026 Worlds promo announcement](https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokemon-news/pikachu-promo-cards-revealed-ahead-of-the-2026-world-championships) and [Paradise Resort release history](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Paradise_Resort_(MEP_Promo_92)): localized MEP092 competitor promo. The English STAFF target also has an exact photograph and staff-specific product tuple. The provider holo label conflicts with exact normal/non-foil inventory and the German physical listing ([Regular finish](https://www.ebay.de/itm/377466687245)); only non-holo is admitted. Other year/language placement variants are not generated.
- [Champions Festival release history](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Champions_Festival_(SM_Promo_231)): seven-language 2019 kit, supported by the German SM231 card face and explicit non-holo tuple.
- [Pikachu ex 150 artwork inventory](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pikachu_ex_(30th_Celebration_150)): native EN/DE panoramas checked for Lucario, Hisuian Zorua, Hisuian Zoroark and Midnight Lycanroc. German Fennekin MEP059 and Zorua SM83 were checked on their existing localized reference faces.

TCGdex JSON and Limitless/localized reference images supply exact face/number
evidence. Generated default variants and language-scaled marketplace estimates
are not admitted as physical release evidence. Marketplace IDs remain suggestions;
this review does not confirm prices or change saved product mappings.
