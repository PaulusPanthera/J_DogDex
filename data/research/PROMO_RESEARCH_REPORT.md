# Promo dates and artwork — DogDex 1.2.3

Reviewed 28 October 2026. This follow-up prioritizes English/German accuracy and preserves the catalog editor and the chosen species scope.

## Changes from 1.2.2

- Corrected **22 individual promo dates**: 21 German and one English. The original promo-series opening date is no longer used for these later distributions.
- Classified **14 existing identities as Full Art** after reviewing their exact regional pictures: six MEP promos and eight regular V-UNION panels. Added two separately evidenced complete jumbo identities with Full Art artwork. Printed **Promo** rarity is retained throughout; the Full Art total rises from 286 to **302**.
- Added **four oversized physical choices**: English/German Hop's Zacian-ex SVP193, plus one complete English and one complete German Zacian V-UNION jumbo.
- Cached the actual complete English/German V-UNION pictures. The two new identities therefore do not increase the missing-picture count.
- Linked the four regular panels and the complete jumbo to their shared regional product. Each product contains five distinct supported physical choices, counted once each.

The resulting catalog has **3,638 records / 4,233 physical variants**, **24 jumbo choices**, **302 Full Art identities**, **116 cameos**, and **3,444 local reference images**. No personal collection is bundled. Original card and printing IDs remain stable; the two complete jumbos receive their own deterministic IDs.

## Artwork and printed rarity

Exact English and German references were visually inspected for MEP059, MEP080, MEP033 and all four SWSH163–166 panels. Their illustrations extend across the card behind the rules text. These are artwork classifications; none of the printed Promo cards is relabelled Illustration Rare, Ultra Rare or Double Rare.

The new complete jumbos also have full-card artwork, independently verified in their native pictures. A normal ex/EX/GX/V mechanic does not automatically create a Full Art classification elsewhere. Existing exact rarity filters, including Double Rare, remain unchanged.

## One complete V-UNION jumbo, four independent regular cards

The manufacturer describes four regular cards plus **one** complete oversized card. The actual German picture has native KP/rules and all four identifiers on the same physical sheet; the English picture has native English text. The complete card uses the display range `SWSH163–SWSH166` with identifier namespace `composite`, and records its four component entry IDs. The range describes the four printed panel identifiers; it is not an invented fifth promo number.

Each language receives one complete-jumbo identity with one oversized printing. The four normal cards retain their existing IDs and separate ownership. Owning the complete jumbo therefore marks **one of five** physical choices collected, and does not mark the four regular cards owned. Backup/restore preserves the complete card independently.

Sources: [English manufacturer](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/product-gallery/pokemon-v-union-special-collection-mewtwo-greninja-zacian/), [German manufacturer](https://www.pokemon.com/de/pokemon-sammelkartenspiel/Produktgalerie/spezial-kollektion-pokemon-v-union-mewtu-quajutsu-zacian/), [actual German complete/regular alternatives](https://www.deckshop.de/products/zacian-v-union-swsh163-166-de), and [actual English complete jumbo](https://www.pokemons.dk/product/zacian-v-union-jumbokort/).

The Hop's Zacian jumbo is a separate size under the existing SVP193 identity in each language, rather than another card identity. The [English manufacturer](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-tcg/product-gallery/hops-zacian-ex-box) and the native German collection identify both sizes explicitly.

## Remaining work

The catalog is still a working research catalog. There are **189 missing pictures**, **124 records without dates**, and **47 reference-only card dates**. Source-group dates remain accepted for 615 of 651 groups, reference-only for 13 and unresolved for 23. Some historical artwork, cameo coverage and physical variants remain unverified.

Known EN/DE printing holds remain the unreleased November Classic Collection Zacian V previews and German McDonald's 2011 Zorua. The two earlier German jumbo candidates remain held where physical evidence is conflicting or uncorroborated. Shared images can still differ by edition/finish and remain manually replaceable.

Eevee and its evolutions, Shinx/Luxio/Luxray and out-of-scope Zygarde forms remain excluded. Japanese/Korean entry records are unchanged; no additional research was done for those languages.

Rebuild with `python3 -S catalog_enrich.py`. The new facts merge into the existing coverage stage; no extra runtime dependencies or new editing workflow are introduced. Validation and package-extraction results are in `VALIDATION.md`.
