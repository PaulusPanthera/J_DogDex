Pokémon-e: Skyridge (Japanese: 裂けた大地 Split Earth and 神秘なる山 Mysterious Mountains) is the name given to the third and final main expansion of cards from the e-Card Series of the Pokémon Trading Card Game. It is the fourth and fifth main expansions from the e-Card Era of the Pokémon Card Game in Japan.

Blurb

Get a Clear Advantage over Your Opponents

The Pokémon®-e: Skyridge™ set has 6 Pokémon with the new Crystal Type Poké-Body power that lets them handle whatever types of Energy you want to deal out. This exciting set also introduces evolved Pokémon with healing abilities that activate whenever you attach Energy, as well as an exciting, new mechanic—Mystery Plate.

Information

Skyridge is the name given to the third and final main expansion of the e-Card Series of the Pokémon Trading Card Game. In Japan, it was released as Split Earth, the fourth expansion and Mysterious Mountains, the fifth and final expansion, in the Pokémon Card Game e-Card Era. The English and German expansions were released on May 12, 2003, and August 15, 2003 respectively, while the Japanese expansions were released on August 23, 2002 and October 4, 2002 respectively.

Similar to Aquapolis, Skyridge also has a separate card collection number for Rare Holo cards. Skyridge was the final expansion to be produced by Wizards of the Coast before the company lost their license. As a consequence of that, there was only one print run of Skyridge, causing its price to skyrocket. It was also the final expansion to include Kadabra cards until the release of 151 in 2023.

Languages this set is released in

The Split Earth and Mysterious Mountains sets are only released in Japanese, in both 1st and unlimited edition. The Skyridge set is released in English, German, and Italian, with all cards except the "H/32" cards also available as Reverse Holos. In a change from Aquapolis, the secret rare cards are also available as Reverse Holos.
